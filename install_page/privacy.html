Privacy Policy

FacePass ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

📷 Camera & Microphone

FacePass requests access to your camera and microphone only during the biometric liveness verification process.

  • Camera is used to capture your face for liveness detection
  • Microphone may be used during the liveness session
  • No photos or videos are stored on your device
  • Camera/microphone are never accessed in the background

📊 Data We Collect

  • Session code — a 4-digit code you enter to start a session
  • Liveness ID — a unique identifier generated by the biometric system after verification
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal identifiers

🔒 How We Use Your Data

  • Liveness ID is transmitted to our secure server to confirm your biometric verification
  • Data is used solely to validate your identity for visa application purposes
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to any third parties
  • Data is stored securely and deleted after the session is completed

🌐 Third-Party Services

FacePass uses the OZ Forensics Liveness SDK for biometric verification. Their privacy policy applies to the liveness detection process:

ozforensics.com/privacy

👶 Children's Privacy

FacePass is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
